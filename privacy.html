Halftide — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Halftide collects nothing. There is no account, no server, and no network request. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Halftide has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It is built entirely on Apple's own frameworks.

Where your data lives

  • Your entries are stored as files on your device, inside the app's own container and a shared App Group container that lets the widgets read them.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Halftide makes no network connections, so your data cannot leave your device through the app.
  • If you use iCloud device backup, your Halftide files are included in that backup, encrypted and controlled by Apple under your Apple Account. We have no access to it.

Health information

What you record in Halftide — how much energy you had, what you spent it on, how you felt — is health-related information. It is treated the same as everything else: kept on your device, never transmitted, never seen by us. Halftide does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Your control

  • Export — Settings lets you export your data at any time.
  • Erase — Settings lets you clear all data. Deleting the app also removes everything; reinstalling over an existing copy keeps it.
  • Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no request you need to send us.

Children

Halftide is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. Should a future version of Halftide ever add a feature that transmits data, that will be stated plainly here and in the app before it ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy: kof2123@hotmail.com

Halftide is a self-reported energy journal, not medical advice.

萤光汐语 — 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 7 月 27 日

萤光汐语不收集任何数据。没有账号、没有服务器、没有任何网络请求。你记录的一切都留在你自己的设备上。

我们收集什么

没有。没有统计分析、没有崩溃上报、没有广告、没有追踪，也没有任何第三方 SDK。全部功能基于 Apple 官方框架实现。

你的数据在哪里

  • 你的记录以文件形式存放在你的设备上，位于 app 自己的容器和一个共享容器内（后者让桌面小组件能读到数据）。
  • 不上传。萤光汐语不建立任何网络连接，因此你的数据无法通过这个 app 离开你的设备。
  • 如果你使用 iCloud 设备备份，萤光汐语的文件会包含在该备份中，由 Apple 加密并归属于你的 Apple 账户。我们无法访问。

健康相关信息

你在萤光汐语里记录的内容 —— 今天有多少精力、花在了什么上、感觉如何 —— 属于健康相关信息。它与其他数据同等对待：留在设备上、从不传输、我们从不查看。萤光汐语不读取也不写入 Apple 健康。

你的控制权

  • 导出 —— 设置里可随时导出你的数据。
  • 清空 —— 设置里可清空全部数据。删除 app 同样会清除一切；覆盖安装则会保留。
  • 因为我们从未收到你的数据，所以没有任何东西需要我们代为删除，你也不需要向我们提交任何请求。

儿童

萤光汐语不面向儿童，且不从任何人（包括儿童）处收集信息。

变更

本政策如有变更，会在此处发布新版本并标注日期。如果未来某个版本新增了会传输数据的功能，我们会在它发布之前，在此处和 app 内明确说明。

联系

隐私相关问题：kof2123@hotmail.com

萤光汐语是自报精力手记，不构成医疗建议。
