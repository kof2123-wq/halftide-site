Halftide — Support

Energy pacing for people whose energy does not run in a straight line.

Something not working, or not making sense? Write to kof2123@hotmail.com. Replies are asynchronous — usually within a few days, sometimes slower. That is by design, not neglect.

Common questions

The widget does not appear when I search for it

Long-press your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Halftide. If nothing comes up, open the app once first — iOS only offers widgets from apps that have been launched at least once since installing.

Why does my number look different on the widget?

Widgets are refreshed by iOS, not by the app, so they can lag the app by a few minutes. Opening Halftide always shows the current figure.

The companion looks grey in the Dynamic Island

The small pill, minimal, and Lock Screen forms are rendered in a single colour by iOS — that is a system rule and applies to every app. Long-press to expand the island and your companion appears in full colour.

Can I change the language?

Halftide follows your iPhone's language and can be switched without restarting the app. English and Simplified Chinese are both fully supported, including the widgets.

Does the soundscape keep playing when the screen locks?

Yes. There is also a timer — no limit, or 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 minutes — which runs in the background and stops on time even while locked. Dimming the screen during rest is a separate switch, off by default, so you can use the soundscape as background music.

Where is my data, and how do I get it out?

Everything is stored on your device only; there is no account and no server. Settings has both Export and Erase all data. Deleting the app removes everything. Reinstalling over an existing copy keeps your history.

I do not have a regular cycle

That is supported as a first-class case, not an afterthought. Choose the non-cyclical rhythm during setup and Halftide predicts from your recent averages, your day-of-week pattern, and the load of the last few days instead of a cycle position.

The starting numbers are not right for me

They are a general shape, not yours — the app says so on the first screen. As your own days accumulate, Halftide moves onto your data. If the baseline itself looks wrong, Settings lets you change it, and after about two weeks the app will suggest a correction on its own if your records point somewhere else.

Why does the app never tell me I did badly?

Because going over budget is information, not a verdict. Halftide is built to describe what happened and what it might mean for tomorrow, and to stop there.

Halftide is a self-reported energy journal, not medical advice. It does not diagnose anything, does not decide anything about medication or treatment, and is not a substitute for a clinician who knows your whole situation.

常见问题

搜不到小组件

长按桌面，点编辑 → 添加小组件，搜萤光汐语。如果搜不到，先打开 app 一次 —— iOS 只提供那些安装后至少启动过一次的 app 的小组件。

小组件上的数字和 app 里不一样

小组件由 iOS 负责刷新，不由 app 控制，所以可能比 app 慢几分钟。打开萤光汐语看到的永远是当前值。

灵动岛里的精灵是灰的

小条、最简形态和锁屏横幅由 iOS 强制单色渲染 —— 这是系统规则，对所有 app 都一样。长按展开灵动岛，精灵就是彩色的。

能换语言吗

萤光汐语跟随 iPhone 的语言设置，切换无需重启。中英双语都是完整支持，包括小组件。

锁屏后声景还会继续播吗

会。还有定时停止 —— 不限时，或 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 分钟 —— 定时器在后台运行，锁屏也准时停。休息时让屏幕暗下来是另一个独立开关，默认关闭，所以你可以把声景当背景乐用。

我的数据在哪，怎么导出

全部只存在你的设备上，没有账号也没有服务器。设置里有导出和清空全部数据。删除 app 会清除一切，覆盖安装则保留历史。

我没有规律周期

这是被当作首要情况支持的，不是补丁。在引导时选择非周期节律，萤光汐语会改用你的近期均值、星期形状和前几天的负荷来预测，而不是周期位置。

一开始的数字对我不准

那是一个大致的形状，不是你的 —— app 在第一屏就写了这句。随着你自己的日子攒起来，萤光汐语会转到你的数据上。如果基线本身就不对，设置里可以改；大约两周后，如果你的记录指向别处，app 会自己提出修正建议。

为什么它从不说我做得不好

因为超出预算是信息，不是判决。萤光汐语的设计是描述发生了什么、这对明天可能意味着什么，然后就到此为止。

萤光汐语是自报精力手记，不构成医疗建议。它不诊断任何东西，不决定任何关于用药或治疗的事，也不能替代一个了解你全部情况的医生。
